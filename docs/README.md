# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

OctoAcme manages cross-functional projects through initiation, planning, execution, release, and retrospective. Teams first confirm the business need, success measures, stakeholders, and initial risks, then turn approved work into a prioritized backlog with acceptance criteria, estimates, milestones, and dependencies. During execution, work is delivered in small increments and tracked on a project board.

The Project Manager coordinates schedules, risks, dependencies, and communications; the Product Manager defines outcomes and priorities; developers implement and test the work; QA validates quality and acceptance criteria; and stakeholders provide input and approvals. Daily standups surface progress and blockers, weekly delivery or PM–Product Manager syncs review status and risks, and milestone demos share results. Stakeholder updates and escalations keep decisions and blockers visible.

Quality is checked throughout delivery with unit and integration tests, code review, CI tests and linting, and manual QA where needed. Before release, teams verify acceptance criteria, CI and security scans, prepare release notes, smoke tests, and a rollback plan, then perform post-deployment checks. Retrospectives after sprints, releases, or incidents turn lessons into owned, trackable improvement actions.
